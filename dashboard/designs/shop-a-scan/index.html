What will my scan cost?

Pick your plan and the service your doctor ordered. We compare in-network sites near you using the rates your insurer publishes, then apply your deductible.

Your deductible numbers stay in this browser. Find them on your insurer's member site or your latest Explanation of Benefits.

Before you book

Call the site and your insurer to confirm they are in network for your plan. Hospital sites may also bill a separate radiologist fee, which we add where the insurer publishes it. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.
