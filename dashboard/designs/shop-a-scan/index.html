Where should I get my scan?

For Captivated Health members. We compare in-network sites near you using the rates your plan publishes, apply your deductible, and show each site's care score.

Your deductible numbers come from your sovereign pod if you allow it, or you can type them. They are never sent to the price service.

Ask a question

The assistant explains numbers worked out by the app's estimate engine. It never makes up a price and doesn't give medical advice.

About the care score

The care score is our own rating from 1 to 5. It combines public Medicare quality measures, such as infections, complications and patient surveys, with reports from members who were treated there. We show it only when there is enough data. Call the site and your plan to confirm network status before you book. All figures are estimates.
